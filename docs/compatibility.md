# Compatibility and historical reports

## MAXmade scope

- Original first-generation MediaNav hardware, originally running 4.x; minimum starting version **4.0.3**.
- The same hardware already converted to **7.0.5.MD** is included.
- Factory MediaNav Evolution and later hardware are unsupported by this full package.
- Old conversion reports do not establish MAXmade installation/runtime compatibility.

## Original conversion reports

These reports came from the former 7.0.5.MD guide:

- **4.1.0:** Renault Clio IV, 2012 model; [issue #7](https://github.com/m-a-x-s-e-e-l-i-g/MediaNav-to-Evolution-Upgrade/issues/7) contains a warning.
- **4.0.7:** reported working.
- **4.0.6:** 2014 Renault Clio IV.
- **4.0.5:** reported by [dgm1g15 in issue #2](https://github.com/m-a-x-s-e-e-l-i-g/MediaNav-to-Evolution-Upgrade/issues/2).
- **4.0.3:** reported working.
- **6.0.3MD:** described as expected to work, not a confirmed result.

## Older downgrade instructions

- The old conversion guide described a separate removal LGU for returning to 4.0.6.
- That procedure was not checked for MAXmade. Restoring applications alone does not reverse a full OS/boot/MCU update.
- The original instructions remain in [Git history](https://github.com/m-a-x-s-e-e-l-i-g/MediaNav-to-Evolution-Upgrade/blob/d20347b8faebd1d54fea403a1ad36af462d67ee0/Upgrade_705MD_FavreMod/README.md#downgrade).
- Current installation, backup, CE/DBoot and MICOM instructions stay in the [main guide](../README.md).
